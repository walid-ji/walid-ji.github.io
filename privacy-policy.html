Ayyati – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Ayyati ("the app") shows verses of the Quran and hadiths, with translations, recitations and daily reminders. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your favorites, settings (language, text size, translation, reciter) and reminder preferences are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Content services

To display verses, hadiths and recitations, the app requests content from alquran.cloud and hadithapi.com. These services receive standard technical information that comes with any internet request, such as your IP address. No personal information is sent to them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and your interactions with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened), Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the app crashes, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings without an update. These services may collect device information, app version, crash details, app instance identifiers and usage data. This information is used only to improve the app. See the Firebase privacy information.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app settings or in your device's notification settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is only processed by the third-party services listed above, under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the app. Data processed by Google services is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
